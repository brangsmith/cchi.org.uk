    MSt in Healthcare Innovation

    Our flagship programme. Three years, part-time, in three stages: PgCert, PgDip and MSt. Open to people from any technical or healthcare-related background.

    Apply

    Learn to innovate across engineering, design and healthcare

    You study how healthcare systems work and the technologies used in them, then apply both to a research project of your own. Residential weeks in Cambridge, online sessions and self-directed study fit around a full-time job.

    Length
    Three years part-time, one stage a year. Breaks are allowed, up to eight years in all.
    Awards
    PgCert, PgDip, MSt. You leave with the highest you complete.
    Your time
    About one day a week.
    Course Director
    Professor James Ward

    What you gain

    • Systems understandingHow local, national and global healthcare systems work, and where technology fits.
    • A shared languageThe vocabulary and mindset to work between medicine, engineering and commercial settings.
    • Leadership skillsSystems leadership, and using technology to deliver value in healthcare, research and business.
    • Industry contextWorkshops, seminars and projects with industrial partners.
    • Sound practiceThe practical and ethical questions behind improving care and developing biomedical technology.
    • A networkClassmates from health, engineering and industry who will lead in this field.

    Pathway

    Each stage stands on its own. You leave with the highest award you complete.

    How it works

    Your time
    About one day a week across the year, in peaks and troughs. It is built to fit around full-time work.
    Residential weeks
    Each PgCert and PgDip unit has a week in Cambridge. Attendance is required.
    Between weeks
    Supervisions, usually online, plus online lectures and resources.
    How you are taught
    Lectures, seminars, small-group teaching, guest speakers, workshops, practicals, case studies and team-building sessions.
    Assessment
    A written report for each unit. Units 1, 3, 4 and 6: one report of 3,000 to 4,000 words. Units 2 and 5: three short reports of four pages each.
    Moving on
    Pass the year to progress. The PgCert and PgDip each work as a stand-alone course.

    The MSt year

    A research project of your own, on a topic in scientific, medical, social or business innovation in healthcare.

    Dissertation
    10,000 to 12,000 words. It can use primary or secondary data, or be a systematic review, quantitative or qualitative.
    Planning report
    Up to 3,000 words. It sets your research in the context of future trends and shows how a systems approach shapes innovation.
    Supervision
    A project supervisor, monthly online meetings, and group seminars on research skills.
    In Cambridge
    Short visits to present your plan and, later, your findings. Other year groups are invited to the final presentations.
    Assessment
    The planning report and the dissertation, each with an oral presentation.
    Joining
    For current or deferred PgDip students who passed the PgCert year. You apply separately for the MSt and for a Cambridge College. The College fee is included in the course fee.

    Entry requirements

    Who it suits
    New graduates and mid-career professionals from any technical or healthcare-related background.
    Degree
    First or upper second class honours, or equivalent. Usually life sciences, medical sciences, computing or engineering. Other backgrounds considered.
    Language
    Non-native speakers need an English language test. Requirements
    Visas
    Overseas students attend on a Student Visitor visa. Student visa sponsorship is not available. Guidance
